# student-attendance-risk-analysis

Data Analytics project to analyse student attendance patterns, identify attendance and academic performance risks, and recommend supportive interventions using Python and machine learning.



\## Project Overview



Low student attendance may be associated with

academic difficulties and the need for additional

academic support.



This project analyses student attendance records

alongside approved academic variables to identify

students who may be at risk of low attendance or

academic performance.



The goal is to support early identification and

recommend appropriate interventions while

protecting student privacy.



\## Problem Statement



Analyse attendance records alongside approved

academic variables. Build models to identify

students at risk of low attendance or performance

and evaluate their reliability. Recommend early,

supportive interventions while protecting student

privacy.



\## Objectives



\- Analyse student attendance patterns.

\- Identify factors associated with low attendance.

\- Explore relationships between attendance and

&#x20; academic performance.

\- Build predictive models for risk identification.

\- Evaluate model performance and reliability.

\- Recommend supportive interventions.
